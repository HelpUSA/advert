# Brand Registry

The brand registry is the first product module in Advert.

## Purpose
Every campaign, content draft, approval and report must belong to a registered entity. The entity can be a company, product, person, service or social profile.

## Initial profiles
- HelpUS BR: company profile
- Advert HelpUS BR: product profile
- Professional Profile: person/profile template
