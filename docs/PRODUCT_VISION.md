# Product Vision - Advert HelpUS BR

## Goal
Build an owned web platform that lets our watcher-powered operation plan, create, approve, schedule, publish and report digital promotion for companies, people, services and products.

## MVP Modules
1. Brand/profile registry
2. Campaign planner
3. Content calendar
4. AI-assisted draft generator
5. Approval queue
6. Publishing checklist and future connectors
7. Asset brief generator
8. Metrics and weekly report

## Free-first strategy
Use local development, Git, Vercel frontend and Railway backend. Avoid paid third-party marketing platforms at this stage.

## Safety boundaries
The system must not automate spam, fake likes, fake comments, fake followers, mass DMs, credential sharing, abusive scraping or impersonation.
